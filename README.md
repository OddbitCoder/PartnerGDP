# Diffs

The following differences were found between the original schema and the original board:

- The original schema does not contain decoupling capacitors. Most +5V/GND connections on ICs are also missing.
- AVDINT- is connected to ST8 (single-pin header), which is missing from the original schema.
- Pull-down resistors R55 and R56 for signal lines ATTD6 and ATTD7 are missing from the original schema.
- R32 is connected to +5V in the original schema but is connected to GND on the board.
- SH3 exists in the original schema but is not present on the board.
- GDP RAM pin numbers in the original schema are mostly incorrect.
- CK64 and CK65 are not in the original schema. CK64 is not installed on the original board; CK65 is a 22pF capacitor on the original board.
- IC17/8 is not connected to AVINT on the original board.
- IC17/9 is not connected to GINT on the original board.

# Mods

## HCK Mod

On the original board, the trace between IC67/7 and IC66/12 is physically cut by a drilled hole between IC66 and IC67. A wire is added from IC67/7 to IC66/4.

This mod is implemented in commits #3f22–#c79c.

## Video Output Stabilization Mod

The document (Odprava-napak-graficne-kartice.pdf, "Tresenje slike na graficnih PARTNERJIH") contains the following steps for stabilizing the text video output:

1. Carefully cut the PCB trace running from pin 13 of IC1 to the via. Be careful: the PCB is a four-layer board.
2. Connect pin 6 of IC11 to pin 8 of IC11.
3. Connect pin 8 of IC11 to pin 10 of IC11.
4. Connect pin 6 of IC11 to pin 9 of IC71.
5. Route the GDPSYNC signal from SH11 - the contact on the IC51 side - to pin 13 of IC51.
6. Connect pin 12 of IC51 to pin 5 of IC11.
7. Route the EOC signal from pin 15 of IC61 to pin 11 of IC51.
8. Connect pin 10 of IC51 to pin 9 of IC11.
9. Route the CLKEN signal from pin 8 of IC1 to pin 11 of IC11.
10. Install a resistor of approximately 300 Ω between pins 10 and 14 (+5 V) of IC11. Anything from 220 Ω to 470 Ω is acceptable.
11. Connect pin 13 of IC1 to pin 8 of IC71.

This mod is NOT implemented on the original board.
